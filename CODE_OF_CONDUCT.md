# Code of conduct

BIXSO OS projects are built in public with people from many countries, trades and backgrounds. We want every issue, pull request and discussion to be a place where people can ask, disagree and learn.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be specific and constructive. Say what you tried, what you expected and what happened.
- Respect privacy. Use fictional data, and never post someone else's personal or customer information.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on scope, even when you disagree, and keep the discussion on the work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Sexual content, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report behaviour that breaks this code through GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or by messaging the moderators of [r/BIXSO](https://www.reddit.com/r/BIXSO/). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock a thread, or block someone from the project, depending on how serious and repeated the behaviour is.

This code applies in this repository and in BIXSO OS community spaces.
